ℹ️ Inline Management

Click on any group row to reveal the accounts assigned to it. Click the ⇄ button on an account to reassign it to another group in 2 seconds.

💡 Safety Rules

  • Groups with active accounts cannot be deleted.
  • Accounts with transactions cannot be deleted; move or archive them instead.
  • Moving an account between groups instantly updates your Balance Sheet calculations.

ℹ️ Category Management

Click any category group to reveal its child categories. Click ⇄ on a category to reassign it to a new parent group immediately.

💡 Transaction Safety

  • Groups with active categories cannot be deleted.
  • Categories with historical transactions cannot be deleted to preserve reporting integrity.
  • Renaming or moving a category preserves all historical transactions via stable category IDs.
